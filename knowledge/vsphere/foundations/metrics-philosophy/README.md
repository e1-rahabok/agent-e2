# Metrics Philosophy

Deep thinking about the nature of metrics in virtualized systems and the broader IT industry.

Content in this folder is primarily derived from Chapter 1 of the metrics book (“The World of Metrics”), especially the section **Metrics Nuances**.

## Files

| File | Topic |
|------|------|
| `01-metrics-as-accounting.md` | Metrics are accounting of system behavior, not just numbers to collect |
| `02-naming-complexity.md` | Inconsistent naming across platforms and products |
| `03-resource-type-differences.md` | Why CPU, Memory, Storage, and Network metrics have different natures |
| `04-volume-of-metrics-problem.md` | The explosion of metric volume and the question of business value |
| `05-interpretation-challenge.md` | Why thresholds are hard and “it depends” is often the real answer |

These documents are foundational. They should be retrieved when the AI needs to reason about *how* to think about metrics, not just what a specific counter means.
