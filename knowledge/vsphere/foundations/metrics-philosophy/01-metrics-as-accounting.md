# Metrics as Accounting of System Operations

**Source**: Chapter 1 – The World of Metrics → Metrics Nuances  
**Last Updated**: 2026-11-02

## Core Idea

A metric is essentially an **accounting of a system in operation**.

To understand a counter properly, you need knowledge of how the entire system works. Without internalizing the underlying mechanics, you are forced to rely on memorization.

Memorizing counters is only useful for passing certification exams. It does not produce real operational mastery.

## Implication

Take time to truly understand the reasons behind the metrics.  
You will appreciate thresholds much better when you know how the value is actually calculated and what it represents in the system.

You are not in the business of collecting metrics.  
You are in the business of solving real-world problems.
