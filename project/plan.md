## Mission
- 1.0 scope: Iwan 'E1' Rahabok's expertise in VCF performance and capacity management, for monitoring and troubleshooting, through a purpose-built agent, harness and knowledge base. What is pushed out of 1.0 is listed in section After 1.0.
- Release: the author decides when to release, from the benchmark questions (Q212, 2026-11-14). Release criteria are not Claude's target: Claude works on the quality of the knowledge and of Agent E2, and keeps the scope and the After 1.0 list current.
- Agent E2, an expert system of agent, harness, knowledge base and an open-weight model, running locally (customer data stays on site; one GPU is the stretch target, not a limit). The benchmark is the proof that it works (MISSION.md).
- Reference numbers for the author's release decision (Q130; [benchmark/design.md](https://github.com/e1-rahabok/agent-e2/blob/main/benchmark/design.md), section 7.5), Agent E2 as shipped (one pipeline, the expert-led agent of [harness/AGENT.md](https://github.com/e1-rahabok/agent-e2/blob/main/harness/AGENT.md); author, 2026-11-14) on the local model: final score at least 85%; Level 400 at least 80%; confident-wrong at most 3%; no up-front misconception (the `## Misconceptions` lines marked `up_front`) stated as true; held-out set at most 11 points below the development set. The gap to the frontier model is an outcome, reported with its paired test, not a gate. Target 99%. Confirmed on the shipped configuration by the live test.
