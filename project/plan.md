- Reference numbers for the author's release decision (Q130; [benchmark/design.md](https://github.com/e1-rahabok/agent-e2/blob/main/benchmark/design.md), section 7.5), Agent E2 as shipped (one pipeline, the expert-led agent of [harness/AGENT.md](https://github.com/e1-rahabok/agent-e2/blob/main/harness/AGENT.md); decided 2026-11-14) on the local model: final score at least 85%; Level 400 at least 80%; confident-wrong at most 3%; no up-front misconception (the `## Misconceptions` lines marked `up_front`) stated as true; held-out set at most 11 points below the development set.
