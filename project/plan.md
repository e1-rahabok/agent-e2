## Mission
- 1.0 minimum: Iwan 'E1' Rahabok's expertise in VCF performance and capacity management; higher score than frontier models on monitoring and troubleshooting through a purpose-built agent, harness and knowledge base. Anything else is not a release blocker.
- Agent E2, an expert system of agent, harness, knowledge base and an open-weight model, running locally (customer data stays on site; one GPU is the stretch target, not a limit). The benchmark is the proof that it works (MISSION.md).
- Release gate (Q130; [benchmark/design.md](https://github.com/e1-rahabok/agent-e2/blob/main/benchmark/design.md), section 7.5), Agent E2 as shipped (one pipeline, the expert-led agent of [harness/AGENT.md](https://github.com/e1-rahabok/agent-e2/blob/main/harness/AGENT.md); author, 2026-11-14) on the local model: final score at least 85%; Level 400 at least 80%; confident-wrong at most 3%; no misconception of misconceptions.md stated as true; held-out set at most 11 points below the development set. The gap to the frontier model is an outcome, reported with its paired test, not a gate. Target 99%. Confirmed on the shipped configuration by the live test.
