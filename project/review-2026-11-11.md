# Mission and benchmark review, 2026-11-11

Not indexed. A big-picture review: is the mission clear and doable, what major components are missing, is the benchmark able to prove the claim, and what earlier expert systems teach. Nothing here is carried out until the author decides (Q163-Q167 in [project/author-questions.md](https://github.com/e1-rahabok/agent-e2/blob/main/project/author-questions.md)). The previous review: [project/review-2026-11-06.md](https://github.com/e1-rahabok/agent-e2/blob/main/project/review-2026-11-06.md).

## 1. Summary
- The mission is clear on what to build. It is not clear on what to prove.
- Doable with a narrower claim. "Better than the best frontier model", unqualified, is hard to defend.
- The architecture is sound. The gaps are a decision engine, evidence handling, field data and benchmark validity. Not folder structure.
- 100 questions are enough to track the 85% gate. Not enough to prove superiority unless the gap is large.

## 2. Mission
### Clear
- Reader: a VMware administrator. Scope: vSphere 9.1 performance and capacity, with the Guest OS layer. Local on one GPU; customer data stays on site.
- The theory in MISSION.md, section 3, holds: open book, a frontier model that memorised the wrong answers, currency, exact values from tools.

### Not clear
- Two missions mixed: capture and teach the author's expertise (a user outcome), and beat frontier models with a 32B model (a benchmark claim).
- No user-outcome metric: time to root cause, right root cause on a real ticket.
- Scope worded four ways: "VCF performance and capacity", "vSphere 9.1 metrics and Guest OS", "private cloud operations", "monitoring and troubleshooting".
- "Frontier model" is undefined. Today: one model, no web search, no extended thinking. Open to the charge of a strawman.

### Doable
- Plausible: the local system beats a frontier model used alone on Level 400 questions in this domain.
- Unlikely: the local system beats a frontier model given the same knowledge base and harness. That is the ceiling, not the target.
- Proposed framing: frontier-class answers without data leaving the site. Beating the frontier model alone is the minimum; matching the frontier model with the knowledge base is the stretch goal.
- Current numbers: Retrieval 77% of needed files in context, 61 of 95 complete. 0 of 267 knowledge files reviewed. The release gate scores the worst of 5 runs, stricter than that average.
- 99% cannot be proved at 100 questions (benchmark/design.md, section 4). Keep it out of the mission headline.

## 3. Architecture and knowledge organisation
### Strong
- Model, agent, harness and knowledge kept apart; the model frozen and swappable.
- Exact values from the catalog and calculator; layers separated; `same_name` trap guard; one place per number.
- Traces, retrieval check and wrong-answer classification make failures diagnosable.

### Weak
- Knowledge weighted to meaning, not operations: 224 of 267 files are metric meaning or how-it-works; 17 are flows. Troubleshooting and capacity are 45% of the benchmark.
- Structure churn without retrieval gain: 298 moved ids; retrieval 72% to 77%. Folder layout barely moves BM25 or embedding ranking. Stop restructuring.
- Highest-leverage retrieval fix: deterministic linking. Map counter names, chart labels and esxtop fields in the question to files through the catalog; search fills the rest. Listed under M3 "Next"; move it first.
- Agentic retrieval is not measured.

## 4. Major missing components
| # | Missing | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decision engine. Today [`harness/flows.py`](../harness/flows.py) serves step text; the model evaluates each branch | Classic expert systems: rules, working memory, inference engine. E2 has rules and uses the LLM as the engine. Code should compare extracted values with catalog thresholds and pick the branch; the model extracts and explains |
| 2 | Case state: object, layer, metric, value, interval, time window, findings | Guided troubleshooting, screenshot read-back and the verifier need it |
| 3 | Evidence handling: table tool, export parsers; screenshot reading on a 32B vision model unmeasured | Level 400 is diagnosis from evidence. 24 questions wait for attachments; table questions cannot be scored |
| 4 | Multi-turn evaluation (simulated user) | The product is guided troubleshooting; the benchmark is single turn |
| 5 | Field-data loop: real tickets, held-out set | 0 real tickets. Questions from the knowledge base measure its recall, not expertise |
| 6 | Knowledge lifecycle: per-fact release applicability; review at scale | 0 files reviewed; the author is the single source |
| 7 | Customer overlay: their thresholds, classes of service, naming | Real answers depend on them |
| 8 | Measured out-of-scope detection | Own-knowledge fallback turns a gap into a confident wrong answer |

## 5. Benchmark
### Is 100 enough
- For the 85% gate: yes, ±7 points.
- For superiority: the test is paired. Power comes only from discordant questions. Level 100-200 add almost none.
- Power of a one-sided exact McNemar test, α 0.05, E2 wrong on 5% of the questions the frontier model gets right:

| Questions | Gap 10 points | 15 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 (Level 400 today) | 35% | 61% | 81% |
| 100 | 68% | 91% | 98% |
| 150 | 85% | 98% | ~100% |
| 200 | 93% | ~100% | ~100% |

- At 10% instead of 5%: 100 questions give 51% power at a 10-point gap.

### Validity, more than count
- 68 of 95 questions drafted by Claude from knowledge files: they test knowledge base recall.
- Question writer and frontier reference are the same family.
- Author-framework questions (SP5, VM Performance (%), Close for Business, Cluster Risk score) cannot be known by a frontier model; losing them proves nothing about expertise. Proposed tag: `knowledge_class: public-mechanism | vendor-current | author-framework`; superiority claimed on public-mechanism.
- One frontier vendor, no web search, no thinking.
- Held-out set does not exist.
- One LLM judge on 30% of the questions; agreement with the author not measured (kappa).

### More Level 400
- Yes, of one kind: diagnosis from evidence, from real tickets, on public mechanisms. More synthetic trap recall adds little credibility.

| Set | Size | Writer | Visible to Claude | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dev (current) | 100 | as now | yes | development, model choice, 85% gate |
| Claim | 150-200, Level 300-400, at least half real tickets | author and 1-2 VCDX peers | no, sealed | superiority, at release |
| Basics | 25, Level 100-200 | as now | yes | regression only |

- Arms: E2 local; frontier alone, 3 vendors, web search and thinking on; frontier with the E2 knowledge base (ceiling).
- Blind pairwise grading by human experts on the claim set's judged answers.
- Do not select questions because the frontier model fails them. An adversarial set, if used, is declared and reported apart from a naturally sampled set.

### Conflict with an earlier decision
- 2026-11-06: no benchmark run until M1 and M2 are complete. The same review said "we stopped measuring".
- Proposal: run the 70 scorable questions now as a development signal: 1 candidate and the frontier reference, 3 modes, about US$ 20-40. Q163.

## 6. Earlier expert systems: lessons
| System | What happened | Lesson for E2 |
|---|---|---|
| MYCIN (1970s) | Matched experts in blind evaluation; never used in clinics | Accuracy is not adoption: workflow fit (VCF Operations, tickets), trust, liability wording |
| XCON / R1 (DEC, 1980s) | Real savings; about 10,000 rules; permanent maintenance team | Maintenance dominates: release applicability per fact; automated update scan |
| Knowledge acquisition bottleneck | Expert time was the limit | The author is the single source: review by risk and citation count; peer reviewers |
| Brittleness at the edge of scope | Abrupt failure outside scope | With an LLM fallback it becomes a confident wrong answer: measure out-of-scope |
| IBM Watson for Oncology (reported 2018) | Synthetic cases from one institution; unsafe recommendations reported | Synthetic is not validation: real tickets needed |
| BloombergGPT (2023) | Domain model soon matched by GPT-4 | The lasting asset is knowledge, tools and evaluation; keep the model swappable |
| Medprompt (2023) | Generalist plus prompting beat a specialist model | Frontier plus domain context is the strong competitor; frame the claim against it |
| Legal RAG tools (Stanford, 2024) | Marketed as hallucination-free; wrong about 17-33% | RAG does not remove errors: claim-level grounding check, independent evaluation |
| RCACopilot (Microsoft, 2023-24) | Collect diagnostics, then the LLM classifies the root cause | Evidence first, then reasoning |
| Dynatrace Davis | Deterministic causal engine; generative layer explains | Code decides the branch; the model explains |
| ITBench (IBM), AIOpsLab (Microsoft), 2025 | Agents on live fault-injected systems solve a minority of scenarios | Static Q&A overstates operations skill: a fault-injected lab scenario test (M5) |

- Possible competitor: Broadcom's own VCF assistant (announced 2025 as VCF Intelligent Assist; to verify). Position E2 against it.
