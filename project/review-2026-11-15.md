# Knowledge base review, 2026-11-15

Scope: `knowledge/` after the answers and lab tests of 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15: structure, overlaps and contradictions, review notes and corrections, how well the files carry the knowledge. Not indexed.

- Method: an inventory by script (kind, size, tags, links, notes, corrections, Misconceptions, relevance line per file), then seven area reviews, each with its own files to edit: CPU, memory, storage with network and esxtop, object types with architecture and the vSphere Client, the method (concepts, performance, observability), capacity with outputs and VCF Operations, and the glossary with the up-front lines. A finding in another area went to that area's reviewer. Authority for a conflict: the current vendor document (vSphere 9.1 documentation and Performance Best Practices 9.1, revision 20260624, in project/sources; Broadcom KB; the vSphere API 9.1 reference), then the author's later answer, then lab results in the files, then older text. What none of them settles became an author question.
- Then checked here: every added line for who-said-it words and the avoided phrases of tags.yaml (none), the glossary changes against the files they cite, the cross-area fixes, lint, both unit suites, the retrieval check before and after.

## 1. Verdict
- Structure: sound; no restructure. The method (sp5-framework) apart from the product, metrics by technology, KPIs, health checks, alerts and flows by object, the Guest OS as its own layer: each still blocks the failure it was chosen for (KNOWLEDGE-ENGINEERING.md, section 5). Only two sections in 288 files exceed 700 words (the glossary, one chunk on purpose; the steps of can-cluster-take-more-vms), so the files chunk as designed. The proposals in section 4 are local moves and merges.
- Overlap and contradiction: real, and mostly from answers applied to one file and not to its neighbours. 27 contradictions settled (section 2); the duplicates found were shortened to a link to the home.
- Capture: the mechanism is there; the gaps were form. Before this review 87 of 95 metric files lacked the relevance line (writing rule of 2026-11-15) and 6 lacked Misconceptions; 7 of 12 up-front lines were wrong, vague or incomplete when read alone. All fixed.
- review_notes were mostly out of date: 465 notes in 201 files, now 123 in 92. Most deleted notes were provenance, "checked against 9.1", or questions already answered. corrections: 64, now 17; 40 of the deleted ones corrected the author's own material, which metadata.md does not allow.
