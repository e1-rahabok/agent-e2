# Questions for the author

Not indexed. One entry per question.

How to answer:

- Write your reply after `Answer:` under each question. Any length.
- Leave `Answer:` empty to skip a question for now.
- Then tell Claude the file is updated. Claude applies each answer to the knowledge base and moves the entry to [author-questions-resolved.md](author-questions-resolved.md).
- Open questions are in two files: [release 1.0](author-questions-release-1.0.md) (must be answered before release 1.0) and [future release](author-questions-future-release.md) (to-do for a later release).
- `Final answer:` (2026-11-12) is the answer proposed for your review. It replaces the drafts of 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-11 and the partly-settled notes: it is checked against the knowledge base as it is now, the decisions in project/plan.md and your answers up to [Q179](project/author-questions-resolved.md#q179--memory-tiering-rows-for-cluster-consumption-cluster-performance-and-cluster-risk--proposal-for-review). Its status says how it was reached: settled (by an earlier answer or decision, named), expert call, or needs you. `For you:` names what only you can give; "nothing" means accept or change. In `Answer:`, write accept, accept with changes, or your own answer.

Each entry has:

- Section: book chapter and Heading 3.
- Files: knowledge-base files affected.
- Search: verbatim strings to find in the book, only for a question about the book. A question about this repository links its files instead.
- Written as now: what the knowledge base currently says.
